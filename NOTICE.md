# Source and adaptation notices

pstack-quant is an MIT-licensed adaptation of pstack orchestration ideas for quantitative financial research. New code and research-specific instructions are authored for this repository.

The adapted source is [michael-denyer/pstack-claude](https://github.com/michael-denyer/pstack-claude), version `0.9.103`, itself a Claude Code port of Lauren Tan's pstack. Source materials reviewed include `skills/poteto-mode/SKILL.md`, its feature and hillclimb playbooks, `references/codex-tools.md`, `references/resume-storage.md`, and the how, show-me-your-work, technical-writing, unslop, and skill-authoring workflows.

The adaptation retains coordinator ownership, bounded assignments, independent review gates, role configuration, evidence-driven resumption, and explicit blocked states. It replaces general software hillclimbing with preregistered research dossiers, trial accounting, temporal data checks, and financial-result limitations. It does not vendor upstream's full skill tree or execution helpers.

Upstream's MIT notices for Lauren Tan and Michael Denyer are preserved verbatim in [licenses/pstack-MIT.txt](licenses/pstack-MIT.txt). The [root license](LICENSE) covers new repository work. Research papers linked in the methodology reference remain with their respective authors. Their text is not redistributed, and their statistical procedures are not implemented by this plugin.
